## 🌑 Crimson Moon — Biome Summary Sheet (Architect Mode Overview)

| Biome | Category | Environment Type | Temperature | Core Concept | Key Features | Narrative Function |
|--------|-----------|------------------|--------------|----------------|----------------|--------------------|
| **Crimson Plains** | Surface | Dry volcanic plateau | Hot (1.8) | The moon’s dominant biome — scorched crimson grasslands and netherrack soil beneath a red sky. | Crimson Nylium surface, Netherrack base, Blackstone stone layer, minimal aquifers, hostile-mob presence balanced by sparse flora. | Represents the heart of the Crimson Moon — harsh, beautiful, and alive with subtle danger. |
| **Crimson River** | Surface | Warm red river valley | Warm (1.3) | A calm, blood-red river system cutting through the plains; serenity amidst desolation. | Red-tinted water, crimson grass shores, red sand and gravel beds, rare kelp and bamboo, uncommon jungle trees. | Symbolizes balance — the planet’s lifeblood, soft and reflective amid heat. |
| **Scorched Delta** | Surface | Volcanic flats | Very Hot (2.1) | Cooling basalt plains where magma once flowed; smoke, glass, and decay. | Basalt and Blackstone base, Magma Blocks, occasional obsidian, thin air and glassy terrain. | Transitional biome — the middle stage of the planet’s thermal evolution. |
| **Lava River** | Surface | Molten river system | Extreme (lava fluid) | Deep, glowing rivers of molten rock forming the planet’s fiery arteries. | Lava channels 4–8 blocks deep, Blackstone and Magma bed, Red Sand edges, Striders, Piglins, and glowing ambient light. | The Crimson Moon’s lifeblood — pure energy in motion. |
| **Crimson Beach** | Surface | Coastal biome | Warm (1.2) | Cooling shoreline connecting red sands to the Warm Ocean. | Red sand beaches with crimson grass inland, gravel underwater, rare coral and kelp, seagrass, gentle slopes. | Symbolizes the planet’s recovery — the first sign of peace and balance. |
| **Warped Oasis** | Surface (micro-biome) | Arid-lush transition | Mild (0.8) | Rare teal basins where life flourishes beneath crimson skies. | Warped Nylium and moss, bamboo, teal-leaved dark oaks, central water lakes, Allays, frogs, glow squid. | Represents renewal and coexistence — the living heartbeat of the Crimson Moon. |
| **Frozen Lava Tubes** | Subterranean | Deep frozen tunnels | Cold (−0.5) | Ancient magma conduits turned to icy caverns beneath the crust. | Deepslate and ice palette, dripstone and amethyst crystals, lava/water pockets, glow squid and bats. | The final chapter of the planet’s story — silence after fire, beauty in stillness. |

### 🔄 Planetary Flow Summary

**Surface Layer (Heat to Life):**  
→ *Lava River* → *Scorched Delta* → *Crimson Plains* → *Crimson River* → *Crimson Beach* → *Warm Ocean*  

**Micro-Biome Crossroads:**  
→ *Warped Oasis* (rarely between Plains, Desert, Badlands)

**Subsurface Transition:**  
→ *Deepslate Peaks* and *Caves* (cooling crustal systems)  
→ *Frozen Lava Tubes* (planetary heart, sealed and silent)

---

### 🌋 Thematic Progression
Each biome reflects a stage in the **Crimson Moon’s thermal and emotional cycle**:

1. **Lava River** — birth of energy.  
2. **Scorched Delta** — cooling chaos.  
3. **Crimson Plains** — surface stability.  
4. **Crimson River** — reflection and calm.  
5. **Crimson Beach** — peace and renewal.  
6. **Warped Oasis** — life’s return.  
7. **Frozen Lava Tubes** — memory and rest.
